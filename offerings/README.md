## Archive contract

An archived offering preserves the public brief and a small machine-readable record of its identity and status. It is evidence of what was published for that teaching period, not a template to edit for a future delivery. New assessment design starts in the private Instructor repository and receives a new permanent assessment ID; an approved public candidate is then copied into a new offering path.
