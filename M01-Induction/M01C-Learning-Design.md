# Learning design

Pattern Classification is organised around a repeated learning cycle:

1. explain the modelling assumptions and decision problem;
2. inspect a worked derivation or baseline;
3. implement or analyse the method on an appropriately scoped example;
4. evaluate normal, edge, and failure behaviour;
5. compare alternatives and identify limitations; and
6. communicate evidence and responsible-use decisions.

## Module categories

- **Orientation:** M01 establishes navigation and learning expectations.
- **Foundation:** M02 reviews mathematical tools used throughout the course.
- **Core:** M03–M06, M08, and M09 establish the principal classification and
  evaluation framework.
- **Advanced:** M07, M10, and M11 extend the framework through stochastic,
  representation-learning, and privacy perspectives.

An offering may adjust the order or depth, but it should identify all selected
modules and prerequisites explicitly.

## Inclusive and responsible learning

Learning material should use readable structure, descriptive links and figures,
captioned or transcribed media where applicable, and alternatives when a tool
or platform creates an access barrier. Data and AI tools must be used in ways
that respect privacy, provenance, academic integrity, and offering-specific
rules.
