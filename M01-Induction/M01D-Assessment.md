# Assessment and evidence principles

Pattern Classification offerings may use different tasks and weights. Current
requirements are authoritative only when published through the learner's
approved institutional channel or active offering page.

## Common assessment families

- A concept explanation or presentation tests accurate synthesis and clear
  communication.
- An applied classification project tests estimation, modelling,
  implementation, evaluation, and responsible design.
- A technical report or reflection tests interpretation, limitations,
  provenance, and communication of evidence.

The common-core alignment is recorded in
[COMMON-CORE.md](../COMMON-CORE.md#constructive-alignment).

## Expected evidence

Unless an offering states otherwise, strong work should make the following
visible:

1. a clearly scoped classification problem;
2. justified data, representation, and modelling choices;
3. a reproducible analysis or implementation;
4. an evaluation design appropriate to the task and data;
5. interpretation of errors, uncertainty, risks, and limitations; and
6. acknowledgement of sources, collaboration, and permitted AI assistance.

Do not submit personal information, credentials, private links, or restricted
data through public repositories. Follow the current offering's approved
submission and academic-integrity requirements.
