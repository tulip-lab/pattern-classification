# Learning support and contacts

Staff names, contact details, consultation arrangements, and communication
channels vary by institution and teaching period. They must be published in the
current offering's approved unit site or offering page.

For questions about public repository content, use the repository issue tracker
without posting personal information, assessment submissions, credentials, or
private institutional material.

For personal circumstances, grades, extensions, or confidential matters, use
the private channel specified by your current institution.
