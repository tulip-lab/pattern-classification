# Welcome to Pattern Classification

Pattern classification studies how observations can be represented, modelled,
and assigned to meaningful categories under uncertainty. The course connects
probabilistic reasoning, statistical estimation, discriminative learning,
evaluation, and responsible use.

Learners are expected to do more than reproduce an algorithm. A complete piece
of work should explain the problem, justify assumptions, implement or analyse
an appropriate method, evaluate evidence, identify limitations, and communicate
what the result does and does not support.

Use the [common-core map](../COMMON-CORE.md) for the disciplinary sequence, the
public Lab repository for practical work, and the relevant offering page for
current delivery requirements.
